Cookie Cutter Generator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Summary

Cookie Cutter Generator does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your designs never leave your Mac.

Your designs and files

SVG files you open and the STL files you export are processed entirely on your Mac. The app has no servers and sends nothing over the network. It can only read and write the files you choose.

File-name suggestions

On Macs with Apple Intelligence, the optional ✨ name suggestion uses Apple's on-device model. The picture of your design is processed on the device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Payment details are handled by Apple; we never see them. The app only checks with the App Store whether your subscription is active. See Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Settings

Your preferences (sizes, options) are saved on your Mac only.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
