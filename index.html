Cookie Cutter Generator

Turn an SVG drawing into a 3D-printable cookie cutter and a matching stamp on your Mac.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? E-mail waleedafifich@gmail.com — please include your macOS version and, if you can, the SVG file.

Getting started

  1. Import an SVG (File ▸ Import SVG, ⌘O) or drop it on the window.
  2. Set the width in millimetres — the height follows the design.
  3. Adjust the cutter and stamp settings and press Generate (⌘G).
  4. Check the 3D preview and export the STL files (⇧⌘E).

Subscription

Designing, previewing and generating are free. Saving STL files needs Cookie Cutter Generator Pro, a monthly subscription with a one-week free trial, sold by Apple through the App Store. Manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings. To restore it on another Mac, open the subscription sheet and choose Restore Purchases.
